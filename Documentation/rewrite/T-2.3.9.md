#### 2.3.9 FR-9: View Personal Schedule

**Table 2-30 — Description of FR-9**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-9 |
| Title | View Personal Schedule |
| Requirement | A teacher shall be able to view their assigned weekly classes and upcoming periods, and edit the timetable where they have been granted timetable permission; a student shall be able to view the timetable of their linked session. Where nothing has been published, the app shall show an informative empty state rather than unrelated data. |
| Source | Teaching staff and students |
| Rationale | Each user needs quick access to the schedule that actually applies to them — the teacher to their own classes, the student to their session. |
| Business Rule | A teacher sees only their assigned periods; a student sees only the timetable of the session their account is linked to. Both read the same published timetable (FR-8). |
| Dependencies | FR-8, FR-27 |
| Priority | High |
