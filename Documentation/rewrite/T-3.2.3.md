#### 3.2.3 State-Transition Models

State models appear in Section 3.6 for the three workflows where the current state decides what may happen next and what other users can see: student account linking, mark-correction review, and publishing college information. A link request moves from pending to approved or rejected, and a later relink or delink downgrades a previous account's stale approval so a record keeps exactly one live link. A submitted mark is locked; a correction request against it stays pending until an administrator approves it — applying the new value — or rejects it, leaving the original in place. Publishable information is unpublished until it is released to its audience: a datesheet through an explicit published state, and calendar events and notifications by the audience they are addressed to.
