Three people wrote the project, and each of them committed under more than one name/email spelling over the course of it — different machines, a mix of full name and handle. A `.mailmap` and a manual pass fold those aliases back to the right person, so one contributor's work shows up as one row rather than four. One caveat on reading this table: a commit count is who committed a change, not a scoring of effort. Commits vary from a one-line fix to a whole feature, and generated build output was checked in at points early on, which would inflate any line-added/removed figure — so the split is reported as commits and focus areas rather than line churn. There are no automated or co-authored commits in the history: every commit is by one of the three team members, none by a bot or a `Co-Authored-By` trailer.
