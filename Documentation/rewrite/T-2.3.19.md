#### 2.3.19 FR-19: View Datesheet

**Table 2-40 — Description of FR-19**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-19 |
| Title | View Datesheet |
| Requirement | An administrator or teacher shall be able to review the datesheets within their authority, and a student shall be able to view the datesheet published for their linked session and semester — each paper listed with its subject, date, time, and room. A draft, unrelated, removed, or unavailable datesheet shall not appear as an active schedule, and where none has been published the student shall see a clear empty state. |
| Source | All user classes |
| Rationale | An exam schedule should reach students on their own device, not only on a physical noticeboard. |
| Business Rule | A student sees only published datesheets, and only the one for their linked session. |
| Dependencies | FR-18, FR-27 |
| Priority | High |
