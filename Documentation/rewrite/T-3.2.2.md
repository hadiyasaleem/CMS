#### 3.2.2 Interaction Models

The interaction (sequence) models are presented in Section 3.6. They cover signing in and role routing, an administrator publishing college information, a teacher marking attendance and entering marks, and a student registering and linking their account. Each one names the participant, the app in use, and the shared service involved, so the same logic reads the same way on mobile and desktop.
