#### 2.3.8 FR-8: Manage Timetables

**Table 2-29 — Description of FR-8**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-8 |
| Title | Manage Timetables |
| Requirement | An administrator, or a teacher granted timetable permission, shall be able to create, view, update, and remove class periods in the master timetable and in each session timetable. A period sets the day, time, subject, teacher, and room — rooms and buildings are chosen from managed lists — and the timetable also allows zero periods, breaks, and merged lectures. Missing assignments and any overlap of the same teacher, room, or session in the same time slot shall be caught before the period is saved. |
| Source | College administration and teaching staff |
| Rationale | One shared timetable is what keeps rooms, teachers, subjects, and sessions coordinated, and it is what the teacher and student schedule views read from. |
| Business Rule | The same teacher, room, or session cannot hold two overlapping periods; the database refuses such a save. |
| Dependencies | FR-4, FR-5, FR-7 |
| Priority | High |
