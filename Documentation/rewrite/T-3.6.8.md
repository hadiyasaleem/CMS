#### 3.6.8 Publication States

A datesheet begins as a draft while its papers are entered, and stays hidden until it is published; only then can its session's students see it. Calendar events and notifications have no separate draft step — they become visible to their target audience as soon as they are created. When any of these is no longer current, it can be archived or removed from active views without touching unrelated college records.
