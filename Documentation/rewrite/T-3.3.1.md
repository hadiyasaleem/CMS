#### 3.3.1 System Context

At the edges of the system are its three kinds of user; at the centre is one shared backend. Administrators and teachers create and update information within their authority, while students mainly view their own and published information, with a few actions of their own — registration, account linking, and profile upkeep. Whether a person is on a phone or a desktop, their app exchanges data with the same backend, so moving between devices never creates a separate record. That backend is the Supabase project: PostgREST serves the college records, GoTrue handles sign-in, Storage holds uploaded files, and a small set of service-role functions carry out admin-only operations — all reached over HTTPS, with Row-Level Security deciding what each signed-in user may read or write.
