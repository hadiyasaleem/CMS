#### 2.3.23 FR-23: View Student Fines

**Table 2-44 — Description of FR-23**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-23 |
| Title | View Student Fines |
| Requirement | A linked student shall be able to see their own fines — each with its category, amount, and reason — alongside their fee information, while administrators and authorised teachers may view fines within their permitted scope. Where no fine exists, the app shall say so plainly rather than showing an unexplained zero. |
| Source | Students, teaching staff, and college administration |
| Rationale | Everyone involved should be able to see which fines were recorded and why, without chasing a paper note. |
| Business Rule | A student sees only the fines attached to their linked record; a teacher sees fines for their assigned classes; an administrator sees all. Fines are informational — the app records them but takes no payment. |
| Dependencies | FR-22, FR-27 |
| Priority | Medium |
