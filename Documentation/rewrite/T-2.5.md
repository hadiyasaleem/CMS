### 2.5 External Interface Requirements

This section sets out how the six applications meet the world around them across four fronts: the **user interface** each role works through, the **software** the apps depend on (the Supabase backend and the libraries behind it), the **hardware** they run on (Android phones and desktop computers), and the **communications** that carry data between the apps and the backend. The detailed screen design itself is covered separately in Chapter 3.
