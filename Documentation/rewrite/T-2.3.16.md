#### 2.3.16 FR-16: View Marks and Semester Results

**Table 2-37 — Description of FR-16**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-16 |
| Title | View Marks and Semester Results |
| Requirement | A linked student shall be able to view their own subject marks — midterm out of 25, sessional out of 15, and the total — alongside their semester results (GPA, CGPA, any supply subjects, and progression). Administrators and authorised teachers shall be able to review results within their permitted scope. Where a mark or result has not been published, or a student was marked absent, it shall be shown as such rather than as a zero or a completed result. |
| Source | Students, teaching staff, and college administration |
| Rationale | Seeing marks and results on a phone removes the wait for manually prepared copies and trips to the office. |
| Business Rule | A student sees only their own marks and results; a teacher sees the records for their assigned classes; an administrator sees authorised college-wide records. |
| Dependencies | FR-12, FR-15, FR-27 |
| Priority | High |
