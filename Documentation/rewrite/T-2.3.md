### 2.3 Functional Requirements

The 33 functional requirements (FR-1 to FR-33) are laid out in the order information moves through the college: access and people, academic structure, daily teaching records, examinations, fees, communication, reporting, and the desktop file actions. Each one names an observable service and gives its source, its purpose, the main rule that governs it, its dependencies, and its priority — the same fields used in the tables that follow. Between them they cover all six applications, and every requirement notes which of them it applies to.
