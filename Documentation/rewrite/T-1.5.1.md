#### 1.5.1 Admin Mobile Application

The college's control panel in a pocket — the full academic structure plus the approvals and reports an administrator needs while away from the office desk:

- **Dashboard** with the headline totals (students, teachers, departments), pending requests, recent notifications, and shortcuts into the common tasks.
- **Academic structure:** departments and their detail, academic sessions, per-semester subjects (the curriculum), class rosters, per-session and master timetables, buildings and rooms, and each session's fee structure.
- **People:** teachers and their individual permissions, other administrators, and student profiles — including issuing and tracking a student's fines.
- **Approvals:** reviewing student account-link requests and teachers' mark-correction requests before either takes effect.
- **Records and reporting:** attendance reports, an Insights view of academic summaries, submitted examination papers, and examination datesheets.
- **Communication:** calendar events and notifications, both viewing them and sending them.
