#### 2.3.12 FR-12: Enter Assessment Marks

**Table 2-33 — Description of FR-12**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-12 |
| Title | Enter Assessment Marks |
| Requirement | An assigned teacher shall be able to enter a score for each student in an approved assessment and submit the complete award list. Midterm marks are out of 25 and sessional marks out of 15 (entered as attendance, assignment, and quiz components of 5 each). The system shall reject negative values, values above the assessment's maximum, incomplete entries, and unassigned classes with clear guidance. Once submitted, the marks lock against direct editing. |
| Source | Teaching staff and college administration |
| Rationale | Marks have to be entered accurately and then protected from quiet, unrecorded changes. |
| Business Rule | A score stays within its assessment's maximum — midterm 25, sessional 15 — and locks after the first successful submission; changing a locked mark then requires an approved correction (FR-13). |
| Dependencies | FR-5, FR-6, FR-8 |
| Priority | High |
