#### 3.6.4 Register and Link a Student Account

A student registers in the mobile or desktop app through GoTrue, then submits a link request to claim their college record: a roll number chosen from the list of unclaimed rolls for their session, together with identity details (CNIC/B-form, name, and date of birth) for a reviewer to check. Until that request is approved the account stays gated — attendance, marks, results, fees, and timetable are all out of reach. An administrator, or a teacher granted the permission, reviews the request and approves it, linking the account to exactly one student record; if that record is later re-linked or delinked, the previous account's stale approval is downgraded so a record never carries two live links.
