#### 2.3.32 FR-32: View and Maintain Personal Profile

**Table 2-53 — Description of FR-32**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-32 |
| Title | View and Maintain Personal Profile |
| Requirement | A signed-in user shall be able to view their account and college profile — the parts their role is allowed to see — update the fields they may edit, and sign out. An invalid edit shall not overwrite valid information, and protected fields (role, account status, and the email that serves as the account's identity) shall stay read-only, changing only through an authorised administrative process. |
| Source | All user classes |
| Rationale | A user needs to confirm their own identity and keep their contact details current, without being able to alter the fields that decide who they are in the system. |
| Business Rule | A user edits only the profile fields permitted for their role; email, role, and status are administrator-managed. |
| Dependencies | FR-1 |
| Priority | Medium |
