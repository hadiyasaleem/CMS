#### 3.6.1 Sign-In and Role Routing

Sign-in follows the same short sequence in all six apps. The login screen hands the email and password to its view model, which calls the shared auth repository; that repository signs the user in through GoTrue and reads the account's role and status from its profile. Only when the account is active and its role matches the app does the matching workspace open — otherwise the screen shows a clear sign-in or access message, without exposing any service address. When the app is reopened, a still-valid session is restored and the same routing runs without asking for credentials again.
