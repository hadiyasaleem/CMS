#### 2.3.1 FR-1: Sign In and Role Routing

**Table 2-22 — Description of FR-1**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-1 |
| Title | Sign In and Role Routing |
| Requirement | A registered administrator, teacher, or student shall sign in with their email and password through their own mobile or desktop app. The system shall reuse a still-valid session when the app reopens, send the user to the home area for their role, and show a clear, safe message — without exposing any service address — when the details are wrong, the account is inactive, the role does not match the app, or the connection fails. |
| Source | All user classes |
| Rationale | Access is decided per role, so every protected action depends on the user's identity and role being settled at sign-in. Getting that right once is what lets the rest of the system trust who is asking. |
| Business Rule | A user reaches only the area for their account's active role. |
| Dependencies | None — every other requirement depends on this one. |
| Priority | High |
