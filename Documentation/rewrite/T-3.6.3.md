#### 3.6.3 Record Attendance and Assessment

A teacher opens an assigned class and subject in the mobile or desktop app, and its view model loads that class's roster from the shared records. For attendance, each student is marked present, absent, or on leave — with an optional late flag on a present student — and the completed register is submitted through the repository; only the assigned teacher can write attendance, while an administrator can read it for reports but never change it. For marks, the teacher enters a first-time score (midterm out of 25, or sessional out of 15) and submits it, after which it is locked. A later change is never a silent overwrite: the teacher raises a correction request, and an administrator must approve it before the recorded mark moves.
