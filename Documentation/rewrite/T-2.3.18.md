#### 2.3.18 FR-18: Build and Publish Datesheet

**Table 2-39 — Description of FR-18**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-18 |
| Title | Build and Publish Datesheet |
| Requirement | An administrator, or a teacher with the manage-datesheets permission, shall be able to build an examination datesheet for one session and semester, schedule each of that curriculum's subjects with a date, time, and a real building and room, review it, and publish it to that session's students. The system shall block publication when a subject is scheduled twice, when two papers fall on the same date within the datesheet, when a room or invigilator clashes with any other datesheet, or when required entries are missing or invalid. |
| Source | College administration and teaching staff |
| Rationale | A published datesheet gives every affected user one approved exam schedule — and, because exam papers attach to its slots, it is also what paper submissions (FR-17) hang off. |
| Business Rule | A datesheet covers exactly one (session, semester); its papers are the session's own curriculum subjects, each placed in a real room; only an administrator or a permitted teacher may publish; a student sees only the datesheet for their linked session. |
| Dependencies | FR-4, FR-5, FR-8 |
| Priority | High |

> **Change from the earlier report:** the datesheet model was reworked (`20260908120000_datesheet_rework.sql`). A datesheet is now scoped to one **(session, semester)** instead of a free-floating title; its papers are drawn from the session's **real curriculum subjects** and placed in **real buildings/rooms** (previously free text); the **sessional exam type was dropped** (sessional is continuous assessment, not scheduled); and conflict checks now run **server-side across all datesheets** (a subject can't be scheduled twice, two papers can't share a date within one datesheet, and rooms/invigilators can't clash across datesheets). Any earlier description of a free-title datesheet or free-text rooms is stale.
