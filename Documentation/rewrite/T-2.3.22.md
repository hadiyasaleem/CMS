#### 2.3.22 FR-22: Issue Student Fine

**Table 2-43 — Description of FR-22**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-22 |
| Title | Issue Student Fine |
| Requirement | An administrator shall be able to issue a fine to an identified student, recording its category, amount, and reason. The system shall reject a missing student, a non-positive amount, or an empty reason. A fine is informational only — the app records that it was issued, along with who issued it and when, but tracks no paid or unpaid status. |
| Source | College administration |
| Rationale | A fine needs a clear, attributable record rather than an informal note passed around on paper. |
| Business Rule | Only an administrator may issue a fine (teachers can see the fines for their own classes but cannot create them); every fine identifies the issuer, the student, the amount, the category, and the reason. |
| Dependencies | FR-6 |
| Priority | Medium |
