#### 4.1.5 Record Assessment Marks

A teacher enters marks for one subject and assessment type — midterm out of 25, or sessional out of 15 (its attendance, assignment, and quiz components of five each, summing to fifteen). A score must be a whole number within that maximum; anything blank, non-numeric, negative, or over the limit is flagged before submission. Once submitted, a mark is locked, and any later change is not a direct edit — it goes through the correction request in Algorithm 6.

**Algorithm 5 — Record assessment marks**
- *Input:* the assigned class, subject, assessment type, roster, scores, and absence selections
- *Output:* confirmed first-time marks, or field-level correction messages
- *Cost:* O(N) time and space over N students

1. Start after the teacher selects an assignment and assessment type.
2. Load the roster, any marks already recorded, and any pending correction requests.
3. For each student without a locked mark:
   a. accept "absent" or a whole-number score within the assessment maximum (25 for midterm, 15 for sessional);
   b. show a correction message for a blank, non-numeric, negative, or too-large value.
4. If any entered value is invalid, keep the form open and stop.
5. Save only the valid first-time marks and lock them once confirmed.
6. Route any later change to Algorithm 6 (mark correction) and stop.
