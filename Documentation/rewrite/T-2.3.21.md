#### 2.3.21 FR-21: View Fee Challan

**Table 2-42 — Description of FR-21**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-21 |
| Title | View Fee Challan |
| Requirement | A linked student shall be able to view their fee challan, built from their linked session's current fee structure, and generate it as a printable PDF. The challan prints three copies on one page — a Student copy, a College copy, and a Clerk copy — each listing the fee heads and the total. Where no fee structure has been published, the app shall show an informative message; opening, downloading, or printing the challan never records a payment. |
| Source | Students and accounts office |
| Rationale | A student should be able to get their challan from their own device instead of making an information-only trip to the accounts office. |
| Business Rule | A student sees only the fee information for their linked record and session; the app presents the challan but never collects payment. |
| Dependencies | FR-20, FR-27 |
| Priority | High |
