#### 2.2.2 Prototype and Cross-Platform Review

The screens were reviewed as working prototypes (collected in Appendix C) to check that every activity had a clear way in, understandable feedback, and a layout that made sense on both a phone and a desktop. The same pass surfaced the desktop-specific needs a phone does not have — choosing, opening, and saving files, exporting, and printing. Throughout, a role's mobile and desktop apps were treated as two ways into the same work, not two separate stores of college information: the desktop apps run on the same shared logic as their mobile counterparts, so a requirement satisfied on one platform is satisfied on the other rather than specified twice.
