### 1.7 Vision Statement

For the administrators, teachers, and students of Govt. Graduate College, Mandi Bahauddin, the College Management System replaces separate paper records, office visits, and noticeboard checks with one coordinated set of six role-based applications — an admin, a teacher, and a student app, each on both a phone and a desktop. It holds the college's administration, the daily work of teaching, and each student's own academic, fee, examination, and notice information on the same shared records, so a figure entered once is the figure everyone entitled to see it works from. Where the present process makes people chase information across registers and windows, or offers only one device or one slice of college life, this system sets out to give all three groups timely, dependable access to the same approved records through whichever device suits the moment.
