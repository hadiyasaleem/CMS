#### 3.6.6 Student Link Request States

A student account starts unlinked. Submitting a claim moves it to pending, and an authorised reviewer either approves or rejects it. Approval opens the student's personal workspace; rejection keeps academic information closed but lets the student send a corrected request. An unlink — or a re-link to a different account — removes access without deleting the college's student record, and it downgrades the previous account's now-stale approval so the record is never left with two live links. The app stays gated in every state except approved.
