#### 3.2.1 Domain and Class Relationship Model

The domain objects fall into people, academic structure, teaching activity, published information, and financial notices. A department contains academic sessions; a session holds its subjects, its enrolled students, its timetable, and its fee structure; teachers are attached through timetable periods (each in a building and room); and attendance, marks, and results tie a student to that teaching context. A user account provides authorised, role-specific access to whichever of these objects the role may see. Figure 3-1 shows the objects and their relationships, and Table 3-2 groups them by responsibility.

**Table 3-2 — Core Object Responsibilities**

| Object group | Key information | Main operations and inputs |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Account role, status, contact details, and the approved link to a college person. | Verify an account at sign-in; open a workspace by role and status; link a student by account and roster identity; sign out. |
| Academic structure | Departments, sessions, semesters, subjects, rosters, buildings and rooms, and timetable periods. | Create a session from department, intake, and shift; assign a subject to a session and semester; schedule a period from class, teacher, time, and room. |
| Teaching records | Attendance, assessment marks, mark-correction requests, and semester results. | Record attendance from class, date, roster, and statuses; enter marks from subject, assessment, and scores; review a correction from the request and decision. |
| College information | Calendar events, datesheets, examination papers, and notifications. | Prepare information with content and audience; publish after validation; open, save, or print using the selected record and device action. |
| Financial | Session fee structures, fee heads, per-student exceptions, and fines. | Build a challan view from session and student; issue a fine with student, amount, category, and reason; show financial notices without taking payment. |
| Reporting | Approved attendance, marks, results, session totals, and progress summaries. | Build a summary from role, academic scope, and filters; return only what the role is permitted to see. |
