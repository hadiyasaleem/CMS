#### 2.3.29 FR-29: Publish Notifications

**Table 2-50 — Description of FR-29**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identifier | FR-29 |
| Title | Publish Notifications |
| Requirement | An administrator, or a teacher granted the send-notifications permission, shall be able to compose a notification with a title and message, choose its audience, and send it. The audience is a role (or everyone) and can be narrowed to a department or a session. Empty messages, a missing title, and an invalid audience shall be rejected. |
| Source | College administration and teaching staff |
| Rationale | Announcements need to reach the right people promptly instead of relying on a noticeboard. |
| Business Rule | Only an administrator or a permitted teacher may send a notification, and every notification names an audience. Delivery is in-app — there is no external push. |
| Dependencies | FR-1 |
| Priority | Medium |
